Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about your stay at Sophie's Guest Rooms.

Do you offer airport/water taxi pickup?

Yes! We can arrange water taxi and airport pickup for a small fee. We will meet you at your location. Let us know your arrival time and we’ll handle the rest.

Is breakfast included?

No, we do not offer a complimentary breakfast but there are very affordable and delicious places to eat on the Island.

Do you help book tours & dive trips?

We handle bookings specifically for Marlin Espadas. We do offer referals for tours but we don't directly book them.

Is the property family friendly?

Yes! We welcome guests of all ages and we are located at a more quiet part of the Island but still close enough to other places to have fun, such as the split.
