Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about your stay at Sophie's Guest Rooms.

Do you offer airpot/water taxi pickup?

Yes! We can arrange water taxi pickup from Belize City and meet you at the dock. Let us know your arrival time and we’ll handle the rest.

Is breakfast included?

Absolutely our island breakfast with tropical fruit, fry jacks/johnnycakes, coffee/tea is included daily.

Do you help book tours & dive trips?

We partner with trusted local guides for Hol Chan, Shark Ray Alley, the Blue Hole, sunset sails, and more.

Is the property family friendly?

Yes! We have family rooms and can provide extra beds/cribs upon request (subject to availability).
